# Think of one real AI application where dictionaries would be useful. Explain why.
A great real-world AI application where dictionaries are highly useful is Natural Language Processing (NLP), specifically for building a Vocabulary-to-Index Mapping (Tokenization). 
When training AI models to understand human text (like ChatGPT, spam filters, or sentiment analyzers), computers cannot understand raw words like "apple" or "robot". They can only process numbers. 
AI engineers use a dictionary to translate every unique word in a language into a specific tracking number.
